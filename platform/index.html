Nothing on this page is a track record. The platform is not currently trading and I am not claiming a validated edge. What it demonstrates is a research process that reaches negative conclusions quickly, records why, and does not quietly recycle them — which is the part of quantitative work that is hard to fake and hard to teach.

01 Why prediction markets

An event contract pays $1 if a specified thing happens and $0 otherwise, and resolves at a known time against a named source. Three properties follow, and together they make the venue an unusually clean laboratory.

  • The price is a probability. Not a discounted cash-flow estimate, not a risk-neutral density to be extracted — a direct, bounded probability statement with unambiguous settlement.
  • The truth arrives. Every position is scored against a realised outcome within days or hours. Calibration against settlement, not P&L, becomes the regime-invariant measure of skill — and it is available at a sample size that equities-style research would need years to reach.
  • There is no drift to hide behind. Before costs E[Δprice] = 0 by no-arbitrage. There is no buy-and-hold premium lending a marginal strategy the benefit of the doubt. Every claimed edge is a strong claim that must beat the full round-trip cost.

The last one is the reason the cost model gets its own section of the case study, and its own audit. On a venue with no drift, the fee function is one side of the only inequality that matters.

02 The research loop

  1. Pre-register. A falsifiable claim, a named edge source (informational / behavioural / structural / risk-premium / liquidity-provision), a prior probability that it is true, a test plan, and numeric success and kill criteria — written before the data is touched, stamped with the code commit and data window.
  2. Test in-sample. Cost-aware expected value at the ask, proper scoring against settlement (Brier, log-loss), event-level block bootstrap, and an explicit count of how many variants were tried.
  3. One-shot out-of-sample. Parameters freeze; the sealed partition runs exactly once. The pairing is enforced by the data catalog rather than by willpower.
  4. Verdict to the registry. Validated or disproven, with the reasoning either way. Disproven strategies are first-class results and are written up in the same detail as survivors.

03 The statistical backbone

These are the pieces that decide whether a number means anything, and each of them exists because something went wrong without it.

Design decisions and the failure each one prevents
DisciplineWhat it prevents
Effective n = rare eventsFor a high-hit-rate strategy the information lives in the losses. Below a minimum loss count, bootstrap CIs are provably overconfident — a loss-free sample resamples loss-free — so intervals switch to Wilson on the win rate
Event-level block bootstrapTrades within one event are one bet. Per-trade t-statistics on clustered outcomes manufacture significance
Decision-time computabilityLookahead lives in trigger definitions, and inflates in-sample and out-of-sample equally. OOS discipline offers no protection; only computability at decision time does
Settlement-based outcomesA loser's final print averages 0.16, not 0. Reading P&L off the last candle quietly shrinks every loss
Calibrated risk thresholdsDrawdown halts are set from a night-block bootstrap of healthy-edge variance, so the false-trip rate is known in advance rather than chosen by feel
Measured CUSUM operating characteristicsA sequential decay detector whose false-alarm rate and median detection delay were never measured is a light that may never turn on

04 Live execution and TCA

Per settled market, the platform decomposes realised performance into an exact identity rather than reporting a single P&L number:

live_net = (won − vwap_quoted_ask)   mispricing at the quote seen
         + (vwap_quoted_ask − vwap_fill)   price improvement (IOC ⇒ ≥ 0)
         − fees_per_contract

The point of the identity is that it separates being right about the price from getting a good fill, and separates both from the cost of the order structure. Attempts that never fill are logged alongside fills, because a fill log on its own cannot measure what the passive structure gave up — and the markets that got away are joined to their settlements to check whether they would have been worth having.

The finding that matters more than any of them

Every one of the six was intra-event, high-frequency, on a flagship series — which is simultaneously the hardest place on the venue to find surviving inefficiency, the place where the quadratic taker fee is worst (it peaks at p = 0.5, exactly where in-game sports and at-the-money crypto live), and the place where a small account's only real advantages — patience, willingness to hold to settlement, willingness to trade obscure things — are worth nothing.

The falsification engine had been working perfectly and returning the same answer six times. That is not six observations about the venue. It is one observation about one urn.

Recognising that changed the research programme from "find a seventh strategy" to "sample a different urn": neglected, long-dated, low-volume markets held to settlement, where the cost bar is decidable before any statistics are run.

06 Architecture

Components are identified by a permanent label for a responsibility, not for a file — CST-1 means "the thing that answers what a trade costs" whether that lives in one module today or four next year. IDs are cited in commit messages, pre-registrations and the state document. They are never renumbered and never reused, because an ID that changes meaning turns every commit message that cites it into a small lie.

Subsystems
LayerResponsibility
ING · IngressStreaming depth, trades and lifecycle over WebSocket; reference data; account truth (fills, balances, fees actually charged); daily universe cross-section. The only layer permitted to know the word "Kalshi"
STO · StorageAn append-only immutable landing zone and a derived, rebuildable curated store — two stores with deliberately opposite properties
MOD · Instrument modelVenue ticker ↔ a claim with a payoff. Book reconstruction from raw messages; lifecycle, relists and settlements
CST · Cost & microstructureWhat a trade actually costs. §07 of the case study is an audit of this layer
RSK · RiskCircuit breaker before every scan, calibrated drawdown halts, CUSUM decay detection on settled outcomes, capital sleeves and Kelly-fraction sizing
EXE · ExecutionIOC limit orders at the quote, one entry per event, a single-writer fence against duplicate scanners, per-attempt logging for TCA

Two operational details are worth naming because both were paid for. A single-writer fence: every heartbeat records instance and git SHA, and if the previous writer of the heartbeat is not the current process, both sides stand down — a mutual standoff that self-clears. This exists because a forgotten laptop process once duelled with the deployed instance over the same account. And an immutable landing zone: raw capture is never rewritten, because market data is not revisable and alternative data is, and a study that reads the latest revision of a forecast is reading the future.
