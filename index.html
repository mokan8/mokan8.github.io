CASE-001 · Complete strategy lifecycle
A strategy that made money and still had to be killed
MLB late-favourite carry · Deployed · Halted · Retired

Pre-registration through sealed out-of-sample validation, deployment, 45 real fills on Kalshi, and a retirement thirteen days later while the sleeve was up 2.4%. The validating backtest entered on minutes-to-expiry — a quantity anchored on realised settlement time, and therefore not computable at decision time. Includes the full TCA, the honest interval, why a sealed out-of-sample split structurally cannot catch this, and an audit of the cost model the whole verdict rests on, which turned out to be wrong in form and is re-derived here with the expected-value effect stated before and after.
